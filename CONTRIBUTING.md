# Contributing

Enigma is research-stage. There's no public implementation to patch yet, so the most useful contributions right now are critical ones.

## Useful

- **Challenges to the evidence states.** A realistic case the six states can't represent, or one where two states can't be told apart.
- **Challenges to the rules.** A case where the precedence order gives a wrong or misleading answer.
- **Methodology critique.** Weak controls, missing baselines, or ways a comparison of Enigma against simpler approaches could be gamed or could mislead.
- **Prior work.** Related systems or literature in evidence synthesis, argumentation, or provenance that this should be compared against.
- **Unclear documentation.** If something took more than one read, that's a bug.

Open an issue. Short and concrete beats long and general.

## Please don't submit

- **Real student information** of any kind: names, records, IEP content, assessment results, or anything else covered by FERPA or similar law. This includes "anonymized" examples that could be re-identified.
- **Copyrighted full text** of research articles. Cite them instead.
- **Proposed benchmark cases with answers.** Held-out evaluation material has to stay unseen. If you have cases you'd like considered, open an issue describing the *kind* of case, and don't include the answer.
- **Credentials, API keys, or configuration** for any service.

## Examples

New synthetic examples are welcome if they are clearly invented and labeled as such, use fictional interventions and studies, and exercise a distinction the current example doesn't.
